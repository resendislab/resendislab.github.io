title = "Computational modeling of metabolic interactions between MCF-7 cells and Fusobacterium nucleatum as a model of intratumoral microbiota in breast cancer"
+++



The intratumoral microbiota in breast cancer has gained increasing attention in recent years, as it has been shown to exhibit greater diversity and abundance compared to other tumor types. Within this diversity, the bacterium Fusobacterium nucleatum has been associated with tumor progression and metastasis through various mechanisms, including metabolic regulation.



In this project, we developed an in silico community metabolic model integrating genome-scale models (GEMS) derived from an MCF-7 spheroid. This model represents three cellular subpopulations with distinct metabolic phenotypes (invasive, reservoir, and proliferative) interacting with F. nucleatum. We used the MICOM framework to investigate metabolic interactions between the bacterium and heterogeneous cancer cell subpopulations, influenced by tumor metabolic dynamics.
