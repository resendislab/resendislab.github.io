title = "Application of Machine Learning Models to Identify Associations Between Gut Microbiota and the fecal metabolome"
+++

The gut microbiota is composed of a rich community of microorganisms, including beneficial, commensals and even potentially harmful ones. Under normal conditions, the gut microbiota maintains a symbiotic relationship with the host, providing multiple benefits. Moreover, due to its metabolic capabilities, it can supply a wide range of molecules and metabolites, thereby maintaining a form of "communication" between the microbiota and the host. Therefore, this master’s project is focused on the integration of gut microbiota and fecal metabolome data using machine learning tools, with the objective of identifying relevant biological associations between the bacteria composing the gut microbiota and the collection of metabolites found in the fecal material.
